========================================
6. HOW TO APPROACH GREEDY PROBLEMS
========================================

Before coding, answer these questions:

1. What is my greedy choice?
2. Why is this choice optimal?
3. What should I sort by?
4. Can I prove that my local decision leads to a global optimum?

Also identify whether the problem requires:
- Sorting
- Two Pointers
- Heap / PriorityQueue
- Stack
- Queue
- HashMap / TreeMap
- Binary Search
